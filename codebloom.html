Our story

We exist to make professional web design accessible.

WebPixle was founded because we watched great small businesses — barbershops, restaurants, contractors, boutiques — lose customers to competitors who simply had a better website. That felt wrong.

We built a streamlined process that lets us deliver high-quality, custom-designed websites at a price that makes sense for a small business owner. No bloated agency overhead. Just great work, fast.

  • You own your site 100% — no lock-in
  • Direct communication, no account managers
  • Mobile-first, Google-optimized from day one
  • Clear timelines and no surprise costs
  • Ongoing support available if you need it
